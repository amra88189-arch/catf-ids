THE PROBLEM

Network-only detection isn't enough anymore

IIoT connects physical machines to networks. An attack that used to just mean stolen data can now mean physical damage — and today's detectors aren't built for that reality.

1

IIoT links physical machines to networks — attacks can cause real harm, not just data loss.

2

All published IDS systems use network traffic only — ignoring sensors, logs, and telemetry.

3

Per-event classifiers miss multi-stage campaigns that unfold across many events over time.

4

Public datasets are ~82% attacks — a headline "99% accuracy" hides the real false-positive cost in production.

OBJECTIVE

One verdict, fused from every signal

Fuse all four signal types into one verdict, catch attacks that unfold across many events — not just one moment — and explain every alert in plain terms security teams can act on.

THE SOLUTION

A 9-layer governance stack behind every verdict

CATF-IDS doesn't just classify a single event — it runs a layered pipeline that scores, fuses, contextualizes, and escalates risk continuously, then explains the result.

01

Layer Models (RF)

Per-modality random-forest models score network, IoT, log, and endpoint data independently.

02

Behavioral Physics

Flags physically-implausible or anomalous device states, not just statistical outliers.

03

Fusion Meta-Classifier

Combines the four modality scores into a single weighted risk verdict.

04

Modality Trust Tracker

Dynamically re-weights each data source based on its recent reliability.

05

Policy Engine

Translates the fused risk score into concrete, actionable responses.

06

Adaptive Threshold

Continuously tunes the alert cutoff instead of relying on a fixed threshold.

07

Contextual Threshold Engine

Adjusts sensitivity based on operational context — site, time, load.

08

Energy FSM

A four-state escalation machine (Safe → Suspicious → Elevated → Critical) that tracks sustained risk over time.

09

Behavioral Memory

Clusters past incidents to recognize multi-stage campaigns as they unfold.

WHO IT'S FOR

Built for the sites that can't afford blind spots

Security teams and facility operators at critical infrastructure sites where a missed intrusion means more than a data breach.
